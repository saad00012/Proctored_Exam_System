1. The Problem We Are Solving

Traditional online examination methods have a fundamental weakness: the institution has no control over what a student does on their personal device during a test.

Without Our System

  • Student minimizes exam to search Google
  • Screenshots shared on WhatsApp groups
  • Disconnecting internet "pauses" the clock
  • Same questions given if student retries
  • No live supervision — cheating found too late
  • Hours of manual MCQ paper grading

With Our System

  • Phone becomes a single-purpose exam device
  • Screenshots produce completely black images
  • Server controls timer — disconnect does not help
  • Brand new question set assigned every retry
  • Faculty watches live with instant intervention
  • Automatic instant MCQ grading on submission

3. Who Can Access the System?

Security begins well before a single question is displayed. Our platform includes multiple verification layers:

⟶

(e.g. @dnyanshree.edu.in)
⟶

⟶
No → ACCESS DENIED

Key Access Controls:

  • Institutional Email Whitelisting: Only accounts under the institution's official email domain are permitted. Personal Gmail, Yahoo, or Outlook accounts are automatically rejected.
  • Phone OTP Verification: Each student profile is linked to a verified mobile number using one-time passwords, preventing impersonation.
  • Device Administrator Permission: Students must grant the app special device privileges to disable the camera, prevent screenshots, and enforce lockdown. Without consent, the exam cannot begin.

Phase 1: Create — Question Paper Preparation

  • Faculty logs into the Teacher Command Center from any computer or laptop.
  • Creates a new exam paper by entering: Title, Subject, and Duration (in minutes).
  • Adds MCQ questions one by one, each with four options and the correct answer marked.
  • Questions can include text, mathematical formulas, circuit diagrams, or medical images.
  • All uploaded images are automatically compressed to ensure fast loading on slower networks.

Phase 2: Schedule — Timetable Configuration

  • Faculty sets the Opening Date and Time (e.g., Monday, 10:00 AM) and Closing Date and Time (e.g., Monday, 11:30 AM).
  • Students attempting to start outside this window are automatically blocked by the server.
  • Multiple papers can be created for the same subject (used for automatic reassignment if a student cheats).

Phase 3: Conduct — Student Takes the Exam

  • Student opens the app, signs in with institutional credentials, and enters the exam lobby.
  • Upon starting, the phone enters lockdown mode: camera disabled, screenshots blocked, AI assistants blocked.
  • Questions appear one at a time. After answering, the student taps "Next" and cannot return to previous questions.
  • A visible countdown timer shows time remaining.

Phase 4: Supervise — Faculty Monitors Live

  • Faculty sees a live table of every student currently taking the exam with color-coded status badges.
  • If a violation occurs, the student's row highlights immediately with warning indicators.
  • Faculty can click to review details and take action (override or malpractice) instantly.

Phase 5: Results — Automatic Scoring and Reports

  • All answers are scored instantly upon submission against the correct answer key.
  • Dashboard displays score distributions, batch averages, and violation history as visual charts.
  • Complete records can be downloaded as CSV/Excel spreadsheets for institutional archives.

5. Intelligent Anti-Cheat: Fair to Accidents, Strict on Cheating

How does the system distinguish between a genuine emergency and a deliberate cheating attempt? It reads real-time hardware signals from the phone's operating system automatically:

SAFE — Zero Penalty

  • Incoming Phone Call — Timer pauses. Student resumes after the call.
  • Screen Lock / Sleep — Timer pauses. Student resumes after unlocking.

No warning recorded. No penalty. Completely safe.

VIOLATION — Exam Terminated

  • Home Button — Leaves exam to home screen.
  • App Switch — Opens browser, calculator, or messaging.
  • Notification Shade — Pulls down notification panel.

Exam closes immediately. Warning strike recorded.

Note
These distinctions are made automatically using hardware-level signals. No manual configuration is needed by faculty, and no student input is required.

6. The Three-Strike Rule: Managing Cheating

When a student commits a violation, the system follows a structured, transparent, and fair escalation process:

STRIKE 1
Student leaves exam to search for answers. Exam closes immediately. Student is signed out. Upon re-login, a completely new question set is assigned. Timer resumes at exact remaining time.
STRIKE 2
Student cheats again on the new paper. Exam closes again. Another new question set is assigned on re-login. Timer continues from exact point of violation.
STRIKE 3
HARD LOCKOUT. The student sees a red blocked screen. They cannot re-enter the exam. The case is escalated to the teacher's review queue.
OVERRIDE
Teacher grants another chance with adjusted time and a fresh question set.
MALPRACTICE
Teacher denies the attempt. Student is permanently disqualified.
Three Key Principles
1. Fresh Questions Every Time — Cheating is futile because the student never sees the same questions again.
2. Exact Time Preservation — If 4:30 remained at cheating, the new paper starts at exactly 4:30. No time gained or lost.
3. Faculty Has Final Authority — The system locks the student, but the teacher decides whether to forgive or fail.

10. Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Do students need expensive phones?
No. Any standard Android smartphone (Android 8.0 or newer) works. No special hardware is required.
Q: What if a student's phone battery dies mid-exam?
The server preserves their exact progress and remaining time. When the student charges and logs back in, they resume from where they left off with the same timer.
Q: Can students cheat by using two phones?
Each student account can only have one active session. Starting on a second device terminates the first. Screenshots are blocked, so there is nothing to photograph from a second device's camera.
Q: What if campus Wi-Fi goes down during an exam?
The server-side timer does not penalize network disconnections. Students reconnect and resume. All previously submitted answers are already saved on the server.
Q: Can the exam be taken on laptops or desktops?
The student app is Android-only by design. Web browsers cannot enforce the hardware-level security locks (camera disabling, screenshot blocking, app-switching prevention) that our anti-cheat model requires.
Q: How many students can take the exam simultaneously?
The cloud infrastructure scales automatically. Hundreds of concurrent students are supported without performance issues.
Q: Is student data secure?
Yes. All communication is encrypted. Student answers are stored in secured cloud databases with role-based access controls. Only authorized faculty can view results.
Q: Can multiple departments use this simultaneously?
Absolutely. Each paper is tagged with a subject and can be independently scheduled. Multiple departments can run exams simultaneously without any interference.
